# PaperBridge AI: Bridging Relevance, Open Access, and Readability in Academic Paper Discovery (Draft v1)

**Abstract:**  
Undergraduate students and researchers frequently encounter three major friction points when conducting academic literature reviews: vocabulary mismatch in traditional keyword search, paywall barriers that obscure essential research, and mismatched cognitive difficulty where foundational learners struggle with hyper-dense publications. We present PaperBridge AI, a unified prototype platform that pairs Sentence Transformer semantic embeddings with dual-stage legitimate open-access discovery and readability profiling. When a user requests a paywalled paper, the system bypasses traditional dead ends by using semantic vector similarity to surface legitimate, freely accessible alternatives on the identical research topic. We evaluate our pipeline across benchmark computer science queries, demonstrating high relevance, robust open-access verification, and scannable visual triage for accelerated literature reviews.
